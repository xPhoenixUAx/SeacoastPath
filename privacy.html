Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeacoastPath s.r.o. collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use seacoastpath.com.

01. Who We Are

This Website is operated by SeacoastPath s.r.o..

We provide digital marketing, lead generation, advertising, and related business support services to companies across Europe and internationally.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), SeacoastPath s.r.o. acts as the data controller in respect of personal data collected through this Website.

We are committed to protecting the personal data of our website visitors, prospective clients, and business partners.

02. Data We Collect

We may collect personal data when you interact with our Website, submit a form, communicate with us, or engage our services.

The types of data we may collect include identity data such as name, surname, company name, and job title; contact data such as email address, phone number, and business address; financial data such as invoicing details, VAT numbers, and billing-related information where applicable; technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, time zone, and system data; usage data such as pages visited, time on site, navigation paths, and interactions; marketing data such as preferences for receiving communications and campaign-related interactions; and business data such as marketing goals, budgets, and project scope.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. If such data is provided, it will be deleted where appropriate.

03. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to respond to inquiries and contact requests, provide, manage, and improve our services, manage client relationships and communications, process invoices and business transactions, analyse website performance and user behaviour, support marketing and advertising activities, ensure website security and prevent misuse, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not process personal data for purposes incompatible with those listed above.

04. Legal Basis for Processing

We rely on the following legal bases under GDPR: contract performance where processing is necessary to provide services; legitimate interests to operate, improve, and secure our business and Website; legal obligation to comply with applicable laws; and consent where required, including for marketing communications and cookies.

You may withdraw consent at any time.

05. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary. Client and financial data may be retained for up to 7 years to meet legal obligations, inquiry data for up to 2 years, analytics data for up to 26 months, and marketing data until consent is withdrawn.

We may anonymise data for analytical and statistical purposes.

06. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell personal data.

We may share personal data with service providers such as hosting, CRM systems, and email tools; advertising and analytics platforms such as Google and Meta; professional advisers such as legal and accounting providers; and public authorities where required by law.

All third parties are required to process data securely and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

07. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers, including Google and Meta, may process personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where such transfers occur, we ensure appropriate safeguards are implemented, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and other legally approved transfer mechanisms.

08. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion ("right to be forgotten"), restrict processing, receive your data through data portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

09. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to ensure website functionality, analyse user behaviour, and support marketing and advertising activities.

For detailed information, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact:

SeacoastPath s.r.o.
Website: seacoastpath.com
Email: support@seacoastpath.com

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in applicable laws, technologies, and our services.

Any updates will be published on this page.
