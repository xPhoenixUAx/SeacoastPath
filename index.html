FAQ

Questions before you start a strategic path?

Most collaborations begin with a focused review of positioning, acquisition, creative, and reporting. These answers explain how SeacoastPath keeps the work practical and easy to move forward.

What type of companies do you support best?

We work best with small and mid-sized companies that already have a real offer, but need clearer positioning, campaign structure, and more useful marketing decisions.

Can you help if we already run paid campaigns?

Yes. We can review the current campaign setup, landing pages, creative angles, analytics, and reporting rhythm, then identify where clarity or performance is being lost.

Do you only provide strategy, or also execution?

Both can be supported. Some clients need a strategic roadmap, while others need campaign architecture, creative direction, and ongoing operating support.

How does a first project usually start?

We begin with a compact discovery stage: business goals, current marketing activity, audience assumptions, key channels, and reporting gaps. From there we define the cleanest first move.
