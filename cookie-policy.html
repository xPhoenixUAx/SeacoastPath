01. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, remember preferences, improve user experience, and provide information to website operators.

02. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to ensure website operation, remember preferences, analyse website performance, measure marketing campaigns, understand traffic sources, and comply with advertising platform requirements.

03. Types of Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are required for core functionality. Analytics cookies help us understand aggregate website usage. Marketing cookies may support advertising measurement and remarketing where consent is provided.
